- **Wedding:** Edit a 5–8-image sequence showing beginning, details, candid emotions and final hero frame. Use the strongest real album and test another Facebook album plus an Instagram carousel. No unapproved third-party music.
- **Portrait:** Curate 5–7 image portrait carousels around personality, styling and expressive close-ups; cross-post with a short BTS-to-final video on TikTok.
- **Brand:** Create before/after concept-to-shoot storytelling for the approved Berbie or other brand campaign, with permission and documented brand credits.
- **Process:** Make short reels led by real people, purpose, light set-up and final output; compare retention and saves with a static photo carousel.
- **Measurement:** After publication, compare per-post **reach, views, interactions, saves, inquiries and confirmed bookings** using consistent 7/30-day intervals, avoid comparing unlike metrics and track clicks to the contact form.
- **Permissions:** Verify people featured, client image publication agreements and brand assets before any repost or paid promotion. Do not launch advertisements, schedule social posts or spend budget based on ranking alone.
