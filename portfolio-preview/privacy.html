Privacy information

This page describes how the Yomi Visuals portfolio's current enquiry interface works. It is not a substitute for a reviewed company-wide privacy notice.

Contact enquiries

The project enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. The portfolio does not submit that form to our database or email inbox. You choose whether to send the message in WhatsApp, whose separate privacy practices then apply.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted using Vercel and images and fonts may be served from external providers. Those providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device details, and request logs for delivery and security. Contact the relevant providers for their privacy details.

Client materials

Photos shown here are examples from the portfolio. Please do not submit confidential documents or sensitive personal information through the enquiry form. Project proposals, contracts and client image handling may be subject to separate written terms.
